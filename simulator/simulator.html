General formula — Level 1
PM = (IM × PN) × VE × SMγ × CPα
MI — Maritime Interests NP — Naval Power SW — Strategic Will MS — Maritime Security CP — Cyber Power γ — MS weight · α — SCMI
Normalized formula — Eq. (1b-1c)
PMnorm = [ PM ]1/neff
neff = 3 + γ + α  ·  PMnorm ∈ (0, 1]
Normalization: geometric mean — UNDP (2010); OECD (2008) The (0, 1] scale is comparable with the maritime power index of Vargas Suárez et al. (2021); this model adds the component breakdown that the latter does not distinguish.

Under normalization, the direct-weight factors (IM, PN, VE) comparten la misma elasticidad 1.21/n_eff−1, mientras que los exponentes γ y α dampen the proportional effect of SM y CP. The strategic priority of CP does not derive from its point elasticity, but from three convergent properties: its brecha (lowest baseline value in the system → greatest absolute margin for improvement), its concavidad (α < 1 → decreasing marginal returns, highest at low cyber maturity) and its transversal enabling character (su degradación compromete simultáneamente PN y SM), a lo que se suma la asimetría de costo y tiempo frente a transformar IM o PN.
