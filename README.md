## 📖 Abstract

The classical formulation of maritime power —the product of **maritime interests** and **naval power**— is insufficient to explain the dynamics of digitalized maritime environments. This project updates it by incorporating **cyber power**, **strategic will**, and **maritime security** as structural variables, within a normalization that makes the result directly interpretable on a five-level scale.

The Colombian case is used as an illustration: it anchors the result on the scale of the most systematic available index and, on that basis, **decomposes** it to show that cyber power is the most efficient investment vector for middle regional powers, given its concave returns at low maturity levels.

## 🧮 The formula

**Level 1 — general normalized expression:**

```
MP_norm = [ (MI × NP) × SW × MS^γ × CP^α ]^(1/n_eff)        with  n_eff = 3 + γ + α
```

| Symbol | Variable | Role |
|:------:|----------|------|
| **MI** | Maritime Interests | The purpose that gives meaning to maritime power |
| **NP** | Naval Power | The instrument (Force × Position × MDA) |
| **SW** | Strategic Will | The decision to employ it (political · institutional · social) |
| **MS** | Maritime Security | The condition of the environment (exponent γ) |
| **CP** | Cyber Power | The proposed structural variable (exponent α) |

The interpretation scale has five levels: **Critical** (0.00–0.20), **Low** (0.21–0.40), **Moderate** (0.41–0.60), **Significant** (0.61–0.80), and **High** (0.81–1.00).

## 🎯 Five original contributions

1. **Cyber power** is formalized as a fifth structural variable of maritime power.
2. **Strategic will** is repositioned as a multiplier of the complete system, not only of naval power.
3. **Maritime security** is disaggregated into three threat dimensions (interstate conflicts, terrorism, blue crime).
4. **Normalization** through the effective number of factors yields an index in (0, 1] with a directly interpretable five-level scale.
5. A **National Maritime Power Index (NMPI)** is proposed as a comparative research agenda, supported by the open-access simulator.

---

## 🧪 Model verification

The model's mathematical properties were verified through **Monte Carlo simulation** (100,000 trials per equation), confirming that every component equation preserves the required (0, 1] range and that the expanded expression is identical to the normalized form.
