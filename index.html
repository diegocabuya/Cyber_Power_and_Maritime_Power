The project

From two variables to five: the sea is also exercised in cyberspace

The classical formula MP = MI × NP does not capture the dynamics of digitalized maritime environments. This research updates it by incorporating cyber power, strategic will, and maritime security as structural variables, with a normalization that makes the result interpretable.

Abstract

The classical formulation of maritime power—the product of maritime interests and naval power—is insufficient to explain the dynamics of digitalized maritime environments. This work updates that formulation by incorporating cyber power, strategic will, and maritime security as structural variables. Through an analytical-conceptual approach and a normalized multiplicative model, the proposal yields interpretable results on a five-level scale. The Colombian case anchors the result on the scale of the most systematic available index and, on that basis, decomposes it to show that cyber power is the most efficient investment vector for middle regional powers. It concludes by proposing a National Maritime Power Index as a future research agenda, accompanied by an open-access interactive simulator that allows the model to be explored.
